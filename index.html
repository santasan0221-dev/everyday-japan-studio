Practical Japan guides, checked against official sources.

Everyday Japan Studio makes short, focused PDF guides for people travelling to — or working in — Japan. Each guide answers one concrete problem: how the new tax-free refund actually works, whether the JR Pass pays off for your trip, what to do about tattoos at an onsen, how much cash to put in a wedding envelope.

We write them the slow way: the facts are checked against official Japanese sources — the National Tax Agency, Japan Customs, JR, the Japan Tourism Agency, established Japanese publishers — and the sources are listed inside every guide. Where something is a custom rather than a rule, the guide says so. Where we could not verify something, it is not in the guide.
